About Amara

A care agency founded on one belief: home is where healing happens best.

For over a decade, Amara has connected families with licensed nurses and caregivers who bring hospital-grade skill into the comfort of home.

Where Amara began
Our Story

Started at one kitchen table, by one worried family.

Amara began when our founder, a home-health nurse, couldn't find a single agency she trusted enough to care for her own mother. So she built one — starting with three caregivers and a shared commitment to treat every client like family.

Today, that same standard guides every match we make, every visit we schedule, and every plan of care we write. We continue to believe that great care starts by listening closely to the people who need it most.

Mission & Values

What guides every visit we make.

Our approach to home care is built around simple principles: treating people with dignity, delivering dependable clinical support, communicating openly, and creating trust throughout every stage of care.

From the first conversation to every visit that follows, these values shape how we listen, plan, communicate, and care for every person we serve.

Patient Safety Commitment

Safety isn't a department — it's every step.

Every part of our care process is designed with safety in mind. From caregiver screening to ongoing supervision, we build safeguards into the everyday experience of care.

  • Multi-stage background checks are renewed annually for every caregiver, helping families feel confident about who enters their home.
  • Registered nurse oversight supports every clinical care plan and helps ensure that changing needs are reviewed appropriately.
  • Scheduled supervisory visits help us review care quality in-home and identify opportunities to improve the experience.

Thoughtful safeguards. Consistent attention.

Because peace of mind is an important part of quality home care.

Our Story

Built around a simple belief.

Amara was created to make professional home care feel more personal, more reassuring, and more connected to everyday family life.

Great care is not only about treatment. It is about feeling heard, respected, safe, and supported throughout every stage of life. That belief continues to shape how we work with every family we serve.

01

Where it started

Care should feel personal.

Amara began with a simple observation: families wanted professional healthcare, but they also wanted the warmth, familiarity, and dignity of home.

Our founder believed that the best care should never make people feel like a number. It should begin with listening, understanding the family, and creating an environment where people feel comfortable asking for help.

02

What changed

Healthcare became more human.

We brought clinical expertise and compassionate caregiving together, creating a service where medical needs and everyday wellbeing are treated as equally important.

Instead of separating healthcare from daily life, we focused on bringing dependable support directly into the home. This allows families to stay involved while receiving professional guidance when they need it.

Every care plan is shaped around the person, their routines, their goals, and the people who matter most to them.

Clinical Support Personal Care Family Support
03

Today

A trusted team, close to home.

Today, Amara continues to connect families with carefully selected professionals who understand that exceptional care is measured not only by outcomes, but by how people feel along the way.

From everyday companionship and personal assistance to more specialized support, our team works to make care feel reliable, familiar, and easy to navigate.

We continue to grow with one goal in mind: helping more families experience the confidence that comes from having thoughtful care close to home.
